About

Useful over impressive.

Ehoro Village is an AI operations and learning-systems firm based in the New York area. We help teams turn AI from a scattered experiment into dependable everyday work.

What we believe

Most teams don't need more AI tools. They need a few workflows that work, data they can trust, and people who know how to use what they have. That's the work we do.

We keep projects small enough to finish, test them against real examples, write down how they work, and make sure a person reviews anything that matters.

FocusAI workflow automation · dashboards & reporting · custom AI assistants · AI training & adoption
