About

Built around
people learning.

Ehoro Village is an AI programs and learning practice in the New York metro area, led by Ade Ijidakinro since 2024. It designs AI workflows that hold up, trains the people who use them, and builds small products to prove the methods work.

What Ehoro Village does

Three kinds of work, one habit: verify, don't trust.

  • AI programs: workflows across several AI tools, with phase gates, decision logs and quality checks
  • Learning: workshops, onboarding and learning modules built from what people need to do
  • Product lab: Lo-Fi Spirits and Rich Alucard, where the methods get tested in public

Ade Ijidakinro

Program Manager, AI & Learning

A first-generation Nigerian-American, born in Minneapolis, raised in Atlanta, shaped by Ann Arbor. Ade studied Political Science and Computer Science at the University of Michigan, then earned an M.S. in Information focused on Human-Computer Interaction.

At Procter & Gamble, Ade coordinated 11 regional project managers on a $20M+ global initiative and received a CEO Award. At the University of Michigan's BRAID program, Ade led research with 100+ community stakeholders and a redesign that lifted sign-ups 40%. Ade also spent nearly four years producing heritage months, seminars and workshops for Michigan's Multi-Ethnic Student Affairs office.

Outside work: music production, oil pastels, games, and learning Yoruba.
