About Ehoro Village

Built around better learning.

Ehoro Village is a privately held learning experience design and education technology company based in New York City. Founded in 2019, the company brings product, learning science, instructional design, research, engineering, delivery, and operations together around one question: what makes a learning experience worth continuing?

What we are

Ehoro designs and builds digital learning experiences for universities and organizations. Work commonly includes learner onboarding, skill pathways, adaptive learning modules, internal authoring and review systems, and the product infrastructure needed to keep those experiences improving after launch.

The team is hybrid-remote and approximately 80–100 people across product and research, instructional design and learning science, engineering, client success and delivery, operations, and leadership.

How we operate

Ehoro tries to run as a product-led organization rather than a traditional content studio. Roadmaps are treated as living documents. Teams use research, learner behavior, delivery constraints, and performance data to make prioritization decisions. The process is intentionally iterative: priorities can change, cross-functional groups form around the work, and not every initiative reaches clean measurement.

How product teams are structured

Product managers typically own prioritization across three to four concurrent initiatives, report to the Head of Product, and coordinate a shifting cross-functional group of roughly eight to twelve people drawn primarily from instructional design, engineering, research, and leadership. Most delivery happens with internal stakeholders, with university subject-matter experts and specialized contractors involved when needed.

Our approach to AI

Ehoro uses AI selectively. Earlier attempts to automate too much of the learning-content pipeline reinforced a simple operating rule: narrow tools with clear review points are more useful than open-ended automation that removes human judgment. The company’s later AI-assisted authoring and review workflows are intentionally scoped and human-in-the-loop.
