Work & Product

Design, delivery, and the systems between them.

Ehoro Village works across learning experience design and product development, from early research and prioritization through launch, measurement, and iteration.

Capabilities

What teams work on.

Engagements differ, but the recurring work is consistent: make the learner journey clearer, make delivery more reliable, and make decisions easier to measure.

Experience

Onboarding & pathways

Onboarding flows, structured skill pathways, adaptive modules, and learning journeys designed to reduce early drop-off and help learners understand what comes next.

Platform

Authoring & delivery systems

Internal tooling and product workflows that support content creation, review, publishing, iteration, and operational visibility.

AI

Assisted—not autonomous

Targeted AI-assisted authoring and review workflows with explicit human review, narrow scope, and quality controls.

Selected measured outcomes

Results with context.

Ehoro does not treat every project as directly comparable. Where programs had clean measurement, targeted improvements produced meaningful movement in retention and production time.

~30–40%

Improvement in 7-day retention on programs where targeted onboarding and experience work was measured carefully.

~3 weeks → ~1 week

Typical production-time reduction on well-scoped projects using the more successful AI-supported authoring workflow.

These figures describe measured outcomes on specific programs and workflows, not a universal guarantee across every engagement.

Product operating model

Ownership across multiple initiatives.

Product managers at Ehoro generally work across several concurrent learning initiatives rather than a single isolated feature area.

Typical PM scope

  • 3–4 concurrent initiatives across a changing roadmap
  • Roadmap and prioritization ownership in partnership with the Head of Product
  • Requirements, sequencing, and delivery across iterative cycles
  • Learner and stakeholder evidence used to inform tradeoffs

Typical working group

  • ~8–12 cross-functional contributors
  • Instructional design + learning science
  • Engineering + research
  • Leadership, university SMEs, and contractors when the initiative requires them
