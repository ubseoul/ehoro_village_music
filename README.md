# Ehoro Village

**Learning Experience Design & Education Technology · New York, NY**

This repository hosts the public web presence and product-lab experience for **Ehoro Village**, a privately held learning experience design and education technology company founded in 2019.

Ehoro Village designs and builds digital learning experiences for universities and organizations, including learner onboarding, skill pathways, adaptive modules, internal authoring/review systems, and AI-assisted learning workflows.

## Company snapshot

- **Founded:** 2019
- **Headquarters:** New York City
- **Operating model:** Hybrid-remote
- **Team size:** Approximately 80–100 people
- **Disciplines:** Product, research, instructional design, learning science, engineering, client success/delivery, operations, and leadership
- **Learner reach:** 10,000+ learners across shipped experiences

## Product organization

Product managers typically own prioritization across **3–4 concurrent initiatives**, report to the **Head of Product**, and work with cross-functional groups of roughly **8–12 contributors** spanning instructional design, engineering, research, and leadership. University subject-matter experts and specialized contractors participate when needed.

## Applied AI

Ehoro uses AI selectively, with a preference for narrow, human-in-the-loop authoring and review workflows over fully autonomous content generation.

## Public product lab

**Lo-Fi Spirits** is a public interactive product experiment exploring persistent progression, collection systems, habit loops, session design, and engagement in a music-driven environment.
