Questions

Good to know

Do you use AI to prepare the workshops?

Yes. AI tools help draft materials and research your organization; a person reviews everything and teaches every session.

Our staff aren't technical. Is that OK?

That's who the workshop is for. Everything is taught on tasks your team already does.

What about privacy?

The session covers what should never go into an AI tool (client data, donor data, health information) and how to set team rules. The policy starter puts it in writing.

How do we pay?

By invoice after the session. Nonprofits receive a W-9 on request.
