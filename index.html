How product works

Cross-functional by default.

Ehoro operates as a product-led learning company rather than a traditional content studio. Roadmaps are living documents, priorities move as evidence changes, and delivery depends on close coordination across disciplines.

Product ownership

Product managers typically own prioritization across three to four concurrent initiatives and report to the Head of Product.

  • Roadmaps & prioritization across multiple learning initiatives
  • Requirements & delivery through iterative development cycles
  • Research & performance data used to guide decisions

Cross-functional delivery

A typical initiative brings together roughly eight to twelve contributors across product, instructional design, learning science, research, engineering, and leadership.

  • Internal stakeholders form the core delivery group
  • University subject-matter experts join when the work requires it
  • Specialized contractors support select projects
Operating principle

Useful learning products come from shipping, measuring, learning, and narrowing the problem—not from treating technology as the outcome.
