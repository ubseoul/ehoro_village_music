Questions

Do you use AI to do the work?

Yes, as a tool. Every project is scoped by a person, and every output is reviewed and tested before delivery.

Will you need our passwords?

No. You receive the code, files and setup steps; anything that touches your accounts is installed by your team.

How long does a project take?

Small automations and dashboards usually take days. Assistants, larger workflows and training programs take one to a few weeks, agreed in the scope.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. We work with teams across the United States, remotely or on site in the New York area.
